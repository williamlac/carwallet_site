CarWallet: Car Costs & Fuel

CarWallet is an iPhone app that shows what your car really costs. Log a fill-up or a charge in seconds (type it, say it, or scan the receipt), add insurance, tax, repairs and other costs, and see your monthly spend, consumption and cost per km. It reminds you when maintenance is due and works out what a trip will cost. Your data stays on your iPhone: no account, no ads, no tracking.
