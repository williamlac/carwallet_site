Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

In short: your car data stays on your iPhone. CarWallet has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We can't see your fill-ups, costs or cars. If you use toll prices (Pro), our server keeps only a random app number, your Pro plan and how many toll lookups you did this month.

Who we are

CarWallet is made by W.S. Lacerda, the one-person business (eenmanszaak) of William Sathler Lacerda in the Netherlands. Our contact details are at the bottom of this page. We are the controller for the little data described under "Our server".

Your car data stays on your iPhone

Everything you log (fill-ups, charges, costs, fixed costs, maintenance, your cars and your settings) is stored only on your iPhone, in the app and its widgets. We don't run a server for it and never receive it.

  • Deleting the app deletes this data. Your iPhone's own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may include it, the same as for any app.
  • When a later version adds iCloud sync, your data will go to your own private iCloud account, which we can't read. We will update this policy before that happens.

No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking

There is no sign-in. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and no third-party SDKs. We don't track you across apps or websites and don't sell or share data with data brokers or advertisers.

Features that use your iPhone's hardware

Receipt scanning (camera and photos)

When you scan a receipt or pick a photo of one, the text is read on your iPhone (Apple's Vision and Apple Intelligence on-device model). The image is held in memory only while it is read. It is never saved and never sent anywhere.

Voice input and Siri

When you tap the microphone, your speech is turned into text on your iPhone (on-device speech recognition), and the numbers are picked out on your iPhone (Apple Intelligence's on-device model, never Private Cloud Compute). Nothing is sent to us. When you use Siri or Shortcuts, Siri's own processing follows Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

Maintenance reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

Location and the Trip tab

The Trip tab can use your current location as the start of a trip you plan, only while you use the app and only if you allow it. To find addresses and routes, the start and destination are sent to Apple Maps (Apple's MapKit), under Apple's privacy policy. The app remembers only your last destination, on your iPhone. You can type a start address instead of sharing your location.

Our server

CarWallet has a small server of its own (carwallet-backend) for features that can't run on your iPhone. Today the app contacts it only when you look up a toll price (Pro) or delete your server data. Free users who don't use those send nothing.

  • The first time (and about once a month after that, or when your Pro status changes), the app sends the record Apple signs to prove the app came from the App Store, and Apple's signed record of your Pro purchase.
  • What the server keeps:
    • The app number from Apple's record (a random number Apple gives your Apple Account for this app; it contains no name, email or Apple ID), the date it was first seen, and whether it came from the App Store or a test build.
    • Your Pro status: which plan (monthly, yearly or lifetime), when it ends, Apple's number for the original purchase, and whether it was refunded.
    • Usage counters per month and feature (for example "12 toll lookups in November"), so one user can't use up the shared allowance.
    These are kept until you delete them: Settings → About → Delete Server Data in the app (see "Your rights").
  • Toll prices (Pro): points along the route (start, destination and a few points in between) and the app currency go to our server, which passes the route points to HERE Technologies to calculate the price. The route is not stored. HERE does not receive your app number or purchase details. Free users never send a route.
  • Logs: the server writes short technical logs (errors and counts) to help us fix problems. They never contain your route, your sign-in token or your full app number (at most its first 6 characters), and are deleted after about 3 days. Your IP address is seen by the server to answer a request; we don't store it, but Cloudflare's logs, kept for the same few days, may contain technical request details.
  • Why, and on what legal basis: to give you the features and the Pro service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), performance of a contract), and to prevent abuse and keep the service running (Art. 6(1)(f), legitimate interest).
  • Who processes it for us: the server and its database run on Cloudflare. HERE Technologies receives only the route points, to calculate the price, under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions, the lifetime unlock and offer codes are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, email address or payment details. Apple tells the app whether you have Pro; the app passes Apple's signed record of it to our server, which keeps the plan and end date (see "Our server").

Export

Settings → Data → Export Data makes a file with your data on your iPhone. It goes only where you send it (Files, AirDrop, Mail and so on).

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. For almost everything, the answer is on your own iPhone: delete an entry, or delete the app. To delete your record on our server (app number, Pro status and counters), open Settings → About → Delete Server Data in the app. Your entries on your iPhone and your Pro purchase stay. If you no longer have the app, email us; because the record holds no name or email, we may ask for the date and plan of your Pro purchase to find it. Questions are welcome by email. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Children

CarWallet is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. A change that sends more data off your iPhone comes with an app update that says so.
