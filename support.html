Questions

How do I back up my data?

Your data is only on your iPhone. To keep a copy, open Settings → Data → Export Data and save the file (for example to Files or iCloud Drive). It contains all your cars and entries as CSV files in one zip. Your iPhone's own backup also includes the app's data.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Restoring your iPhone backup brings CarWallet and its data along. Or export on the old iPhone (above), then on the new one open Settings → Data → Import Data and pick the file. Import only adds what is not there yet.

I bought Pro, but the app says Free

Open Settings → CarWallet Pro → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with. A purchase waiting for Ask to Buy approval unlocks once it is approved.

How do I redeem a code?

Open Settings → CarWallet Pro → Redeem Code and enter the code. You can also use the link that came with the code, or the App Store: tap your picture, then Redeem Gift Card or Code.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings → CarWallet Pro → Manage Subscription, or on your iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Pro stays on until the end of the paid period.

What does Pro add?

More than one car, and toll prices on the Trip tab. Everything else is free.

Why don't I see a toll price on the Trip tab?

  • "Toll price unavailable right now": you are offline, or the toll price service is busy. Try again later.
  • "Monthly toll lookups used up (100)": each Pro user has 100 toll lookups a month (about 50 round trips). They reset on the 1st.
  • "Update CarWallet to see toll prices": install the latest version from the App Store.
  • "cost unknown": the service couldn't price this route, for example a toll it doesn't know.

The rest of the trip is still calculated.

How do I delete my data?

Delete entries in the app, or delete the app to remove everything on your iPhone. We hold no copy of your car data. If you used toll prices, our server keeps a random app number, your Pro plan and this month's toll lookup count; delete it with Settings → About → Delete Server Data. See the Privacy Policy.
